# Fidelity Modern Design System

## Brand & Style
The design system adopts a clean, reliable, and modern corporate aesthetic. The overall mood is precise, accessible, and structured, utilizing **Inter** for all typography to ensure pristine legibility across all platforms and form factors.

## Colors
- **Primary Color (`#deebf8`)**: Soft pale blue for primary surfaces and subtle container backgrounds.
- **Secondary Color (`#47e464`)**: Vibrant green for positive states and successful actions.
- **Tertiary Color (`#b0d928`)**: Fresh chartreuse accent for highlights and data visualization.
- **Neutral Color (`#00bafe`)**: Vivid sky blue for structural and text contrast tones.

## Typography
- **Font Family**: Inter (Headline, Body, Label)
- **Hierarchy**: Defined from large 32px bold headlines down to 11px micro labels, optimized for both desktop clarity and mobile readability.

## Layout & Spacing
- **Grid**: Responsive layout with 1.5rem gutters and 2rem outer margins.
- **Spacing Scale**: Consistent incremental spacing (`space-xs` through `space-xl`) based on standard design tokens.

## Elevation & Depth
- **Depth Model**: Tonal layers combined with soft ambient shadows and low-contrast outlines to establish clear spatial hierarchy.

## Shapes
- **Roundedness Level 2**: Standard UI components feature a 0.5rem corner radius, providing a modern, approachable, and balanced geometry.

## Components
- **Buttons, Inputs, Cards, and Lists**: Standardized with consistent corner radii, clear typographic scales, and deliberate color applications.
